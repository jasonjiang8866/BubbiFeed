Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. FeedMyBaby has no account and no cloud sync, so your logs are private by design. See the Privacy Policy.

What happens to my data if I uninstall the app?

Uninstalling removes all FeedMyBaby data from the device permanently. If you want to keep a record, take screenshots or note totals before uninstalling. A data export feature is on our roadmap.

How do I switch between two babies?

Add each baby in Settings → Baby Profiles. On the Home screen, tap “Switch to…” to change the active baby instantly.

How do I change millilitres to ounces?

Settings → Measurement Preferences → tap the ML/OZ toggle.

I forgot to start the nursing timer — can I still log a feed?

Yes. In Log Feed → BREAST, switch to MANUAL INPUT and enter minutes per side. You can also back-date any feed using the time picker.

Does the app work offline / in airplane mode?

Yes — completely. FeedMyBaby never needs an internet connection.

Is FeedMyBaby free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.

How do I delete a feeding log or a baby profile?

Logs: History → tap a log → delete. Profiles: Settings → Baby Profiles → . Deleting a profile also deletes that baby’s logs.

Tips for night feeds

Turn on dark mode (follows your system setting), and use the 3 AM Speedy Presets on the Home screen to log a bottle in two taps without waking up fully.
