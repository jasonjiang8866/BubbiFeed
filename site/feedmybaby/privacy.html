Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 16, 2026 · App: BubbiFeed (iOS & Android) · Publisher: Nfusia

BubbiFeed (“the App”) is a baby feeding tracker developed by Nfusia (“we”, “us”). We built the App with a simple principle: your family’s data belongs to you, and it never leaves your phone.

1. Data we collect

None. The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information with us or with any third party. The App has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, and makes no network calls.

2. Data stored on your device

To provide its features, the App stores the following information locally on your device only (using the device’s private app storage):

  • Baby profiles — name, birth date, gender, and birth weight that you choose to enter.
  • Feeding logs — breastfeeding durations, bottle amounts and type (formula or breastmilk), solid food descriptions, timestamps, and optional notes.
  • App preferences — active baby, volume unit (ml/oz), and theme settings.

This data is readable only by the App on your device. We cannot access it, and no server stores it.

3. Data deletion

You are always in control of your data:

  • Delete individual feeding logs from the History screen.
  • Delete a baby profile (and all of its feeding logs) from Settings → Baby Profiles.
  • Delete all App data at any time by uninstalling the App.

4. Third parties

The App does not integrate any third-party services that receive personal data. The App may open external links (for example, to documentation) only when you explicitly tap them; those websites are governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for parents and caregivers of infants. The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Because all data remains on your device, no child’s data is ever transmitted to us.

6. Security

Because data never leaves your device, its security relies on your device’s own protections (screen lock, OS sandboxing, and full-disk encryption provided by iOS and Android). We recommend keeping your device’s security features enabled.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles data in a future version, we will update this page and revise the “Effective date” above. Any version of the App that transmits data off-device would only ship together with an updated policy and your explicit consent.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email support@nfusia.com.
